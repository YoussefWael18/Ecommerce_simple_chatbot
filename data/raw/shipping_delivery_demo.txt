DEMO DATA ONLY — Fictional e-commerce support policy; not a real retailer's terms.
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Expedited shipping takes 1–2 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates may change during holidays. A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. If a parcel never arrives after the estimated delivery window, contact demo support with the order number and tracking link so the carrier can investigate. If a delivery address needs changing, contact support before dispatch; changes cannot be guaranteed after dispatch. If the parcel arrives damaged, take photographs of the packaging and item and contact support within 48 hours of delivery.
